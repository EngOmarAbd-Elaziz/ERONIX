# ERONIX v1.0.6

## Android Improvements & Features
- **Performance Optimization**: Incremental rendering for waiting room seats, doctor cards, treatment rooms, and event timeline — eliminated full-container `removeAllViews()` rebuilds on every simulation tick.
- **Version Display & Update Notifications**: Shows the current app version in the header, footer, and settings, plus automatic background update notifications when a new release is available.
- **Simulation Completion Notifications**: Posts a notification when the simulation completes or stops.
- **Swipe Navigation & Tab Icons**: Added icons to the bottom navigation bar and horizontal swipe gestures to switch between tabs.
- **Header Progress Bar & Keep Screen On**: Live completion progress bar in the header and keeps the screen awake while a simulation is running.
- **Saved Settings & Haptic Feedback**: Automatically remembers your last simulation settings, adds subtle haptic feedback on Start/Stop, and asks for confirmation before resetting results.
- **Direct Report Sharing**: Share exported `.docx` reports directly via Android's Share sheet.

## Word Report (`.docx`) Enhancements
- **Embedded Graphical Charts**: Reports now include embedded PNG bar charts for Patient Outcomes and Treatment Duration Histogram alongside styled summary tables and key statistics.
